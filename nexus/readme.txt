Mantella Living World - Followers
Version 1.0.0 by itsPigeon2000

Mantella actions for Nether's Follower Framework: NPCs join or leave your party, wait, follow, stay out of fights or fight freely, and wear clothes you hand them.

Requirements
- Mantella Living World - Integration Hub
- Mantella
- Nether's Follower Framework

Installation
1. Install the hub, then this module.
2. In the Mantella UI's action settings, turn off Mantella's own Follow, Unfollow and Wait.
